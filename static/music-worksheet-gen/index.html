Tips & Notes

Under Notes to use, click the note buttons to choose which pitches appear, or drag a notehead up or down to set a problem by hand.

Click a notehead to add an accidental (cycles ♯ → ♭ → natural).

Answers update automatically as you edit.

Saved Setups store your options (type, clef, difficulty, notes, and directions) — loading one makes a fresh set of random problems from those settings.

Saved Worksheets store the exact problems shown, including notes you dragged or accidentals you added, so you can reload and reprint the same sheet later.
